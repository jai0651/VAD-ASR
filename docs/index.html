Pipeline architecture

The wire protocol, the session state machine, why barge-in dictates the entire threading model, latency budgets, and what is shared versus per-session.

Model loading and sharing, quantization, the event-loop rule, p50/p95, health checks, scaling, telephony realities, and an honest list of what this repo simplifies.

Part 2

The Models, In Build Order

Each one from scratch, each with the production alternate it is measured against.

2

Voice activity detection

A 55K-parameter conv net, hysteresis, pre-roll, and the endpointing layer beginners miss. Includes the live-mic bug and the retrain that fixed it.

Take-away: same architecture, different data, bug fixed — for small speech models the data is the model.
3

Speech recognition (CTC)

The alignment problem, CTC's blank trick, prefix beam search, encoder-decoder ASR, streaming versus utterance-based, and why inference engines exist.

Take-away: CTC's conditional independence is exactly why it produces "mianthes".
4

Speech synthesis

ASR mirrored: attention must decide durations. Guided attention, stop tokens, Griffin-Lim phase recovery, and the modern lineage to Kokoro.

Take-away: a decode-time fix for an exposure-bias problem measured worse — the honest fix lives in training.
7

Noise suppression

A streaming denoiser written by hand from MCRA + decision-directed SNR + log-MMSE/OM-LSA, the two bugs that cost 5 dB of speech, and the measurement.

Take-away: denoising helps the VAD at every SNR and hurts Whisper at 0 dB.
Part 3

Bringing It To The State Of The Art

Replacing the 2015 designs with the 2026 ones, so the remaining gap is data.

10

The modern recognizer

Conformer with relative-position attention, BPE subwords, hybrid CTC/attention, streaming by dynamic chunk masking, and the full training recipe.

Take-away: the architecture halved the error on identical data, ran 2.7× faster, and added streaming for 3%.
12

What we are training, exactly

The full mathematics of the architecture and both loss terms — front end, subsampling, the macaron block, relative-position attention, the chunk mask, the CTC forward recursion, label-smoothed cross-entropy — each with why that step is there and what breaks without it. Every tensor shape and parameter count measured from the code.

Take-away: warmup is not decoration — 1,500 versus the reference 15,000 is what collapsed the model.
11

The neural vocoder

Predicting phase instead of iterating toward it. Vocos-style ConvNeXt plus an exact inverse STFT, and the metric that was rigged in Griffin-Lim's favour.

Take-away: Griffin-Lim is a ceiling no amount of data can lift, because the limit isn't in a learned component.
Part 4

The Field, And What To Build In It

Where the state of the art actually is, and where a small team can still win.

8

State of the art (2026)

The best open models per stage and why those architectures won. The five distinct gaps to production — they are not all "data". Open-dataset training recipes, and where a voice-AI company should actually invest.

Take-away: it's data for VAD, architecture for TTS, and everything at once for ASR.
9

Beating Smart Turn on telephony

A build plan for an 8 kHz-native end-of-turn detector: the documented open bug proving the gap is real, the trap that would invalidate the claim, the telephony augmentation pipeline, and the benchmark protocol.

Take-away: the benchmark is the contribution — and publishing where you lose is what makes it credible.
